Syllabus
      Unit No.                                   Contents                                   Hours.
                    Foundations of HCI and Human Factors
                    Definition, scope and importance of HCI, Evolution of
                    interaction: command line, GUI, touch, voice and AI-based
                    interfaces, The human as information processor: perception,
                    memory and mental models, Human error: slips and mistakes,
         I                                                                                 4 Hours
                    Norman's design principles: visibility, feedback, constraints,
                    mapping, affordances and signifiers, Gulfs of execution and
                    evaluation, Fitts' law and Hick's law.
                    Case study: Good and bad interaction design in everyday
                    products and public kiosks (ATM / ticket vending machine).
                    Interaction Design and Prototyping
                    User-centred design and design thinking (empathise, define,
                    ideate, prototype, test), Shneiderman's eight golden rules and UI
                    design patterns, Visual design: layout, Gestalt principles, colour
                    and typography, Information architecture: navigation design and
         II                                                                                4 Hours
                    card sorting, Design systems and platform guidelines (Material
                    Design, Apple Human Interface Guidelines), Wireframing, low-
                    fidelity and high-fidelity prototyping.
                    Case study: Wireframing and low-fidelity prototype of a
                    university student portal / mobile app.
                    Usability Engineering and User Research
                    Usability engineering lifecycle, Usability and User Experience
                    (UX), User research methods: interviews, surveys and
                    observation, Affinity diagrams, personas, scenarios and user
                    journey maps, Hierarchical task analysis and the Keystroke-Level
        III                                                                                4 Hours
                    Model (KLM), Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics and the ISO 9241-
                    11 definition of usability, Heuristic evaluation with severity
                    ratings and cognitive walkthrough.
                    Case study: Heuristic evaluation of a real-world application
                    (e-commerce / banking / government service app).

Syllabus – Laboratory
Suggested List of Assignments:
Suggested tools: Figma / Balsamiq, Google Forms, card-sorting tool or paper cards, WAVE /
Lighthouse accessibility checkers, NVDA screen reader.
   Assignment No.                                     Contents                               Hours.
                        Exploring Human Factors in HCI
                        Identify and analyse at least two examples of good and bad
                        interaction design (apps / websites). Map Norman's design
          1                                                                                 2 Hours
                        principles (visibility, feedback, constraints, mapping,
                        affordances) to the examples. Write a short reflection on how
                        human error affects usability.
                        User Research, Personas and Task Analysis
                        Design a short survey / interview (5–7 questions) for a chosen
                        application and conduct it with at least five users. Synthesise the
          2             findings using an affinity diagram, and create personas, scenarios  4 Hours
                        and a user journey map. Perform hierarchical task analysis of a
                        key task and estimate the expert task time using the Keystroke-
                        Level Model (KLM).
                        Information Architecture and Navigation Design
                        For a content-heavy site (e-commerce, news or university portal),
                        conduct a card sorting exercise with 2–3 users to group and label
          3                                                                                 2 Hours
                        content categories. Create a sitemap from the results. Evaluate
                        whether the navigation supports findability and efficiency, and
                        suggest redesign improvements.
                        Wireframing and Prototyping
                        For a simple application (food ordering, bus booking or college
                        portal), create low-fidelity wireframes for at least three screens.
          4                                                                                 6 Hours
                        Convert them into an interactive high-fidelity prototype in Figma
                        that applies Shneiderman's golden rules, Gestalt principles and a
                        design system.
                        Usability Heuristic Evaluation
                        Evaluate a website or mobile app against Nielsen's 10 usability
          5             heuristics. Rate each heuristic, assign severity ratings to the     4 Hours
                        violations, and carry out a cognitive walkthrough of key tasks.
                        Suggest redesign recommendations.

                     Usability Testing
                     Plan and conduct a think-aloud usability test of the prototype
         6           from Assignment 4 with 3–5 users. Record task success rate, time          4 Hours
                     on task, error rate and SUS score. Summarise the findings and
                     suggest improvements.
                     Accessibility Evaluation
                     Audit a public website for accessibility (colour contrast, alt text,
                     keyboard navigation, ARIA labels) using automated checkers
         7                                                                                     4 Hours
                     (WAVE / Lighthouse) and a screen reader (NVDA). Compare the
                     findings against WCAG 2.2 and suggest improvements for
                     diverse users.
                     Mini-Project
                     Design, prototype and evaluate a user interface by applying the
                     complete user-centred design process. Suggested topics (not
                     limited to):

                     •   Accessible redesign of an e-governance service app.
                     •   Campus navigation / student services mobile app.
                     •   Conversational chatbot interface for student queries, with
         8                                                                                     4 Hours
                         usability evaluation.
                     •   Appointment booking app for elderly users.
                     •   Voice-based interface for visually impaired users.
                     •   E-commerce checkout redesign validated through A/B
                         testing.
                     •   AR-based campus or museum guide prototype.
                     •   Banking app redesign that removes dark patterns.
